The Westminster, Camden, Lambeth, Southwark and Wandsworth geometries were copied from the provider's simplified dataset, without inventing boundaries. Choice marker points were computed inside their polygons for usability. They are selection anchors, not authoritative town-centre coordinates. Original shape IDs are retained. Boundary matching maps the Westminster feature to the existing player label City of Westminster.

These are simplified local authority polygons, not a legal boundary authority or building footprints. Other stages retain markers unless licensed, reviewed geometry is supplied. The content's editorial status is separate from geometry provenance; it has not been marked human-approved automatically.
